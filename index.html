Product Roadmap

Active
Phase 1

MVP Development & Beta Launch

Months 1–3

Build and deploy the MVP with two core AI models: Fall Detection and Medication Adherence, including app, backend, edge integration, and alert system.

Key Deliverables
  • Dataset collection, annotation & preprocessing
  • Fall Detection & Medication Adherence model training
  • Backend, APIs & application development
  • Model integration with backend & edge devices
  • Event pipeline, dashboard & alert system
  • Medication tracking & activity logging
  • Video capture & playback
  • Testing, bug fixes & beta launch
Outcome: Beta-ready MVP with real-time fall detection, medication monitoring, alerts, and dashboard.
Upcoming
Phase 2

Feature Expansion & Model Improvement

Months 4–6

Improve model accuracy and expand user and monitoring features.

Key Deliverables
  • Multi-patient support (2–3 per account)
  • Family access with view permissions
  • SMS fallback for critical alerts
  • Weekly activity reports & trend analysis
  • Fall Detection & Medication model improvements
Outcome: Improved accuracy, usability, and multi-user support.
Upcoming
Phase 3

Platform Scaling & Advanced Features

Months 7–12

Scale platform for healthcare providers and enable advanced integrations.

Key Deliverables
  • Facility dashboard for care providers
  • Wearable integration (Apple Watch, Fitbit, sensors)
  • Real-time video streaming (WebRTC)
  • EHR system integration
  • Predictive analytics & advanced insights
Outcome: Scalable, enterprise-ready healthcare monitoring platform.
